Is it ever okay to lie? Come argue about it.

A free philosophy workshop for kids. No lectures. No wrong answers. Just good questions and better reasons, argued out loud in a small group.

Format

How it works

01

We start with a question, not a lesson. Something like: is it fair to punish someone for an accident?

02

Kids build the argument themselves, in small groups. They test ideas on each other before anyone tells them the answer, because there usually isn't one.

03

Adults facilitate. They ask follow-up questions. They don't lecture, and they don't grade anyone's opinion.

Who's running it

About the workshop

A

Armaan

Workshop lead

Armaan is a student at the Peddie School, where he edits the school's humanities journal and helps run the Philosophy Club. He's spent the last couple of years studying moral philosophy closely, from Kant to social contract theory, and started this workshop to bring the same kind of arguing he does in class to younger kids, minus the jargon.
